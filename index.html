About

Arrubio is the artist project of Moroccan artist Bilal Hafa, based in Antwerp. Blending alternative pop and R&B with soul, funk and indie rock, his music combines warm synth textures, layered guitars, deep grooves and expressive vocals.

Drawing inspiration from artists such as Michael Jackson, Marvin Gaye, Stevie Wonder, Usher and Tame Impala, Arrubio creates music that moves between groove-driven songwriting, atmospheric production and emotional storytelling.

Live, Arrubio performs as an artist with a fixed four-piece band, bringing the songs to life through expressive vocals, live instrumentation, controlled improvisation and a strong sense of atmosphere.
