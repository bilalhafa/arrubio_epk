About

Arrubio is the artist project of Moroccan artist Bilal Hafa, based in Antwerp. Blending alternative pop and R&B with soul, funk and indie rock, his music combines warm synth textures, layered guitars, deep grooves and expressive vocals to create music that feels both nostalgic and contemporary.

Rooted in a love for artists such as Michael Jackson, Stevie Wonder, Tame Impala and Steve Lacy, Arrubio brings together groove-driven songwriting and emotionally direct storytelling. His songs move between danceable, melodic arrangements and more introspective moments, with warmth, melancholy and connection at the centre.

Arrubio performs with a four-piece live band, bringing the songs to life through tight arrangements, dynamic vocals, live instrumentation and space for improvisation. The result is an energetic yet intimate live experience built around groove, emotion and audience connection.
